# Exective Summary
Driver drowsiness is a safety critical problem as it causes unsafe driving conditions for the driver by lowering reaction times, reduced attention, and worse decision making. There is an estimate that 17.6% of fatal crashes in the United States from 2017 to 2021 have involved a drowsy/tired driver which is about 30000 deaths throughout that time period. We are looking to build a more proactive system that reduces fatigue-related accidents. Our system takes in visual data of the driver and in real time makes inferences based on the driver’s body language. This makes sure that the driver is prone to make less mistakes as it will keep the driver in check to make sure that they are not tired nor drowsy during their drive. We will have fleet operators as an extra precaution to make sure that if a driver is drowsy and not stopping to rest that the driver will then be reminded that they should rest so that an accident does not occur and this will keep the driver safe and the roads safer.

# Need Statement
Vehicular accidents are caused by people feeling too drowsy/tired.

# Goal Statement
Actively prevent people from driving while drowsy.
